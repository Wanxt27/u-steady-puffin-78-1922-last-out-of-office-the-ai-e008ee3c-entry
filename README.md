`main.ts` (Node 22+, stdlib only) writes exactly one line — 229 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Thank you for your email. I have quit to become a barista and can now serve only three customers per minute. For urgent matters, please contact my replacement — also me, but slower, holding a milk jug, and retraining on oat milk.

One sharp, specific idea: a machine habit turned into a coffee-shop habit. The rate limit becomes a serving limit ("only three customers per minute"), and the redirect at the end of a real auto-reply is itself the joke — the replacement is also me, just slower, holding a milk jug, and retraining on oat milk. No espresso, bean, or caffeine pun: the laugh comes from the machine's limits, not from coffee wordplay.

Run it with:

```sh
node main.ts
```
